Pocket plasma

Ions (orange) and electrons (blue) spiral around a magnetic field pointing straight out of your screen. Electrons are lighter, so they circle faster and tighter, and the other way round.

Tilt the phone (or drag a finger) to add an electric field. Everything drifts sideways to it at the same speed, whatever its charge: the E×B drift.

The ∇B slider makes the field stronger at the top (slide right) or bottom (slide left). Now ions and electrons drift in opposite directions, which is a current. Tap 0 to make the field uniform again.

The B slider sets the field strength: stronger field, tighter circles and slower drift (it goes as E/B). ⊙/⊗ flips the field into the screen, which reverses the spin and the drifts.

Tap anywhere to add a pair of particles.
